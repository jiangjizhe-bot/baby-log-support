Tap Baby Log – Help & Support

A simple log for your baby's feeds, diapers and sleep, in English and 中文.

How it works

  • Feed: one tap saves the time. Then add breast milk or formula and the amount if you like.
  • Diaper: tap through a few questions: pee, poop or both, amount, color and texture.
  • Sleep: tap to start, tap Wake up to stop.
  • Tap any entry to change its time or details, or to delete it. Use ‹ and › to see other days.
  • Set the baby's name and birth date in Settings (the gear) to see the age in days.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your entries. See the privacy policy.
