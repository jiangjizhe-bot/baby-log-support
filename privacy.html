Privacy Policy – Tap Baby Log

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Summary

Tap Baby Log does not collect any data. Everything you record stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The feeds, diapers, sleep, weight, temperature and baby details you enter are saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. The developer never receives them. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Sharing

The app does not send your records anywhere. If a future version offers syncing between family members, it will use your own iCloud account through Apple, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes all of its data.

Children

The app is meant for parents and caregivers. It does not collect information from or about children.

Health information

The app is a record-keeping aid, not medical advice. Ask your doctor or nurse about anything that worries you.

隐私政策 – Tap Baby Log

Tap Baby Log 不收集任何数据。你记录的内容只保存在你的 iPhone 上。

你输入的喂奶、尿布、睡眠、体重、体温和宝宝信息只保存在本机的应用存储中，开发者无法获取。没有账户、统计分析、广告或追踪。

应用不会把记录发送到任何地方。如果以后的版本提供家人之间同步，将通过你自己的 iCloud 账户进行，并会先更新本政策。

你可以在应用中删除任何记录。从 iPhone 删除本应用会删除它的所有数据。

本应用仅用于记录，不是医疗建议。有任何担心请咨询医生或护士。
